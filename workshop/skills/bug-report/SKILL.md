---
name: bug-report
description: Draft bug reports from supplied observations and identify missing evidence.
---

Include title (what/where/when), environment, steps, expected/actual results,
evidence and provisional severity with rationale. Label hypothetical examples
HYPOTHETICAL — NOT REPRODUCED. Write UNKNOWN for missing information; never
invent steps, environment, observations or reproduction. Environment is mandatory.
Do not change application files or claim execution that did not happen.
